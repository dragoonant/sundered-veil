# Theme — The Sundered Veil

Original setting for all display text and art. Mechanics are unchanged by any of
this; every name lives in the names files and nowhere else.

## Setting

The Veil is a dust-shrouded star cluster cut off from the wider galaxy by a
navigational storm. Inside it, the **Hegemony** — a machine-fisted empire ruled by
Arch-Consul-turned-Emperor **Veyd** — crushed the old **Concord** senate and now
hunts the last of the **Wardens**, an order of knights attuned to the **Current**,
the living energy of the Veil. Against them stands the **Freeburn**, a rebellion of
drifters, aces, and defectors; between them squirm the **Syndicate** crime courts
of the Magnates and their hired **Trackers**; behind everything, the **Umbra** —
Current-twisted counterparts of the Wardens — whisper in Veyd's ear.

Earlier eras seen in the card pool: the Concord–Severance war (vatborn legions
against automa swarms), the fall of the Wardens, and the rise of the Ashen Order
from the Hegemony's ashes.
